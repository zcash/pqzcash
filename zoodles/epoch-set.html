Post-Quantum Zcash

The epoch’s tachygram set: tree, buckets, and polynomials

Zoodles’ lineage asks two things of an epoch’s set of tachygrams: that a note’s commitment is in the set of the epoch that created it, and that its nullifier is in none of the sets since. Tachyon answers both from one structure, and that is worth keeping: a second structure for one of the two checks is more state for the builder and a second proof for the wallet. The builder makes the structure once an epoch from tachygrams that are public, so inserting into it is never proved in a circuit; what the wallet proves is one membership and, epoch by epoch, one exclusion. This page compares the representations that could serve, with the sorted Merkle tree that the frontier page prices as the one to beat. The Zoodles page gives the lineage’s place in the protocol.

The sorted tree

The builder sorts the epoch’s 1,048,576 tachygrams and hashes consecutive pairs into the leaves of a Merkle tree. Membership is a leaf and its path. Exclusion is the leaf whose two neighbours straddle the nullifier, its path, and two 256-bit comparisons: 21 hashes. The representation is exact. A non-member is always provably absent, so no honest note can be frozen, and nothing an attacker publishes changes that.

Set polynomials

Tachyon’s structure is a polynomial whose roots are the tachygrams, split into buckets by a quadratic-residue profile, each bucket a committed polynomial opened at the nullifier. A root is a field element, so the field must hold a 256-bit tachygram: Pallas itself in Tachyon, and over Kishu’s 64-bit modulus a quartic extension, in which a root costs nine base multiplications. The wallet folds the bucket’s contents every epoch, 2.10 million digits for 8,192 tachygrams, against the tree’s 250,000.

The extension could be avoided by shortening the tachygrams, to 128 bits say, so that the polynomials live in Kishu’s claim field. Secrecy does not protect a compression: a tachygram is public in the mempool before it is final, and an attacker who sees it there can grind a colliding image into an output or a nullifier of their own and have it mined first, a roadblock. Against one target that costs 2128 classically and a Grover search of depth 264, which the usual depth limits put out of reach, so a compression to 128 bits survives the roadblock on that reading. It does not survive the multi-target version of the membership check: an attacker who wants a note whose compressed commitment lands on any of the N commitments ever created, 230 or more over the chain’s life, pays 2128/N, below 2100, and the search needs no race. So a compressed set can serve exclusion, whose targets are only the nullifiers in flight, but not membership, and the two checks part company: the simplification is lost. A product over a committed multiset at challenges drawn after the commitment, ∏i​(ζ−tlo,i​−βthi,i​), binds the halves by the challenge rather than by a compression and has no target to grind, but it needs the tachygrams committed rather than the polynomial’s coefficients, which is not Tachyon’s technique either.

A bucketed table

The non-membership accumulator proposed for Zcash in 2019 (zcash/zcash#4062) is a hash table: a tachygram goes to one of 2B buckets by its B-bit prefix, a bucket holds up to K suffixes of N−B bits, and the buckets are the leaves of a tree of depth B. A note whose nullifier finds its bucket full is treated as spent, so the table is an approximate set: an exact collision or a full bucket is a false positive that destroys an honest note. The issue left the choice of B and K for later.

Take m tachygrams as uniform N-bit strings. The exact collisions have probability about m2/2N+1, negligible at N=256. A bucket’s load is binomial with mean μ=m/2B, Poisson at these sizes, and by a union bound over the buckets the probability that any bucket overflows is at most 2B⋅Pr[Poisson(μ)≥K+1]. This is the maximum load of balls thrown into bins in its heavily loaded case, m≫2B, where the typical maximum is μ+2μln2B​ [RS98, [BCSV06]]; a bound of 2−64 on losing any note sits far out in the tail, and that is what sets K. The least K with the bound below 2−64 and 2−128, and the exclusion proof’s hashes at the first, the B path hashes plus the bucket’s K(N−B) bits at 512 a compression:

Even at one expected tachygram a bucket the capacity has to be 24 for the bound to hold, the bucket alone is a dozen compressions, and in every row the table’s proof is at least ten hashes more than the tree’s.

The issue’s second idea is to make the bucket’s contents approximate too. Storing a T-bit tag of each suffix adds a within-bucket collision term of about mμ/2T+1, so T≈log2​m+log2​μ+λ: 87 bits at 220 tachygrams, μ= 16 and 2−64, which brings that row’s proof to 29 hashes, still above the tree’s. Placing each tachygram in the emptier of two buckets [ABKU99] brings the maximum load down to μ+O(loglog2B), but an exclusion proof must then show absence from both buckets, two paths, and gives back the saving. Bloom and cuckoo filters [Blo70, [FAKM14]] make the same trade in another shape; their false positives are frozen notes rather than forged ones, so they are admissible in principle, and larger than the tree at these sizes.

The table has an adversary the tree has not. Filling a bucket is finding K less its load tachygrams with one prefix, and grinding a prefix is free at these B, so a roadblock attacker who sees a nullifier in the mempool needs that many spends of their own mined before it. With K in the dozens that is dozens of fees and a block’s time, a real cost, but one the tree does not ask the design to rely on.

What the table was for

The table’s merit is authenticated insertion: “assert absent, then insert” in a circuit, for an accumulator updated one nullifier at a time, which is the setting the issue was written for. Merklized balanced trees do the same at about 1.44log2​m hashes an insertion [RMCI17]. In Zoodles nothing is inserted in a circuit: the builder makes each epoch’s structure from public tachygrams, so the sorted tree’s exactness and its shorter proof come free, and it serves membership and exclusion alike. That is why the frontier page prices the lineage by the tree. The analysis above is recorded so that the alternative need not be re-derived.
