Post-Quantum Zcash

Recovery from Tachyon

Tachyon is designed to be quantum-private today and quantum-sound after an upgrade [Tac26]. Zoodles is that upgrade, defined here.

Tachyon’s owner field is a Poseidon commitment over Pallas, pk=Com(ak,nk), to the spend validating key and the nullifier deriving key. Its note commitment cm=Com(pk,v,ψ;rcm) is a Poseidon commitment, with no constraint needed on how rcm is derived, and its nullifiers are hash outputs. So a quantum computer learns nothing from the chain, and the commitment and nullifier parts of this section’s definition hold by design. The only curve points on chain are the value commitments and the randomized keys, and re-randomization keeps those private too. What a quantum computer can do is forge: take the discrete log of a randomized key and authorize a spend, or break the discrete-log polynomial commitments of the proof system. Tachyon’s book sketches a full upgrade, a post-quantum signature proved in zero knowledge in place of re-randomization and a lattice folding scheme in place of Pedersen, and leaves it there.

What the key structure lacks

Recovery before or across that upgrade is the question for Zoodles, and there Tachyon’s key structure has a gap that Ironwood’s does not. The witness for Tachyon’s quantum recoverability is the opening of pk, the pair (ak,nk). Tachyon has no viewing keys as such, but nk plays the part of one: a host that scans for a hardware wallet holds it to derive the nullifiers, and holding it must not authorize a spend. And ak is the public output of a FROST group’s key generation. So once discrete logarithms fall, the recovery witness is in the wrong hands in both settings: a single-signer hardware wallet’s host holds nk and ak, so it could spend; and a FROST group has no secret that only its members hold, since ask is shared and never reconstructed and ak is known to every participant. ZIP 2005 avoids both by committing to a separate qk=Hqk(qsk) whose secret is in no viewing key, and by having a FROST group derive qsk from a privately agreed value [ZIP-2005]. Tachyon has no counterpart, a regression from Ironwood for single-signer hardware wallets and FROST groups alike.

The suggested change

The fix is small. The proof-authorizing key becomes (ak,nk,qk) with qk=Hqk(qsk), ZIP 2005’s BLAKE3 derivation, and the owner field pk=Com(ak,nk,qk) stays one hash commitment: Tachyon’s sponge absorbs four field elements a permutation and pk takes three, so qk fits as the fourth, entered as one Pallas element by reduction modulo p, since a 256-bit hash output is not one. The ordinary spend circuit is unchanged, since it opens pk as before with qk as one more field. The recovery statement proves knowledge of qsk and nk rather than of ak and nk, and a FROST group derives qsk from its privately agreed value as ZIP 2005 prescribes, with ak still the distributed key generation’s output. The cost is one more field in the commitment and, in the recovery proof, one BLAKE3 compression, 11,344 Kishu digits, which the recovery circuit application already charges for Hqk, and the reduction’s range check of 256 bits.

The migration transaction

A Tachyon note moves to Zoodles by a recovery spend and a Zoodles output. The transaction reveals the total it spends from the Tachyon pool, for the pool accounting that the Zoodles page sets out. The spend proves knowledge of qsk against qk in pk, the Poseidon openings of pk and cm over Pallas, the membership of cm in a hash-based tree over Tachyon’s published commitments, and the nullifier’s derivation and its absence from Tachyon’s published nullifiers. It cannot rely on Tachyon’s set polynomials, which are Pedersen commitments, once discrete log is gone, so the tree is the structure for Tachyon’s notes as it is for Zoodles’ own epochs. The shape is ZIP 2005’s Recovery Statement over Tachyon’s note format, with Poseidon where Orchard has Sinsemilla. The frontier page prices the migration as an application, in Zoodles’ proof tree with a recovery node for each input. With Kishu the recovery spend is 1.3 million digits, by limbs, of a transaction’s 1.4 million. Recovering one note takes 0.0599 s of prover on one core with zero knowledge, and four notes in one transaction 0.148 s. The spend derives and excludes one epoch’s nullifier. A note’s nullifiers for the other epochs it has lived through need a lineage of their own, as Zoodles’ notes have, which is not priced.

Tachyon’s own circuits are priced there too in each post-quantum proof system. They are not what such a system would prove, but they compare it with Ragu on the same application.
