# Recovery from Tachyon

Tachyon is designed to be quantum-private today and quantum-sound after an upgrade [Tac26](#ref-tac26). Its owner field is a hash commitment, $\mathsf{pk} = \mathsf{Com}(\mathsf{ak}, \mathsf{nk})$ to the spend validating key and the nullifier deriving key; its note commitment $\mathsf{cm} = \mathsf{Com}(\mathsf{pk}, v, \psi; \mathsf{rcm})$ is a Poseidon commitment, with no constraint needed on how $\mathsf{rcm}$ is derived; and its nullifiers are hash outputs. So a quantum computer learns nothing from the chain, and the commitment and nullifier parts of [this section's definition](spend-authorization.md#quantum-recovery) hold by design. The only curve points on chain are the value commitments and the randomized keys, and re-randomization keeps those private too. What a quantum computer can do is forge: take the discrete log of a randomized key and authorize a spend, or break the discrete-log polynomial commitments of the proof system. Tachyon's book sketches a full upgrade, a post-quantum signature proved in zero knowledge in place of re-randomization and a lattice folding scheme in place of Pedersen, and leaves it there. Zoodles is that upgrade, defined here.

## What the key structure lacks

Recovery before or across that upgrade is the question for Zoodles, and there Tachyon's key structure has a gap that Ironwood's does not. The witness for Tachyon's quantum recoverability is the opening of $\mathsf{pk}$, the pair $(\mathsf{ak}, \mathsf{nk})$. Both are full-viewing-key material, and $\mathsf{ak}$ is the public output of a FROST group's key generation. So once discrete logarithms fall, a full viewing key is a spending key, and a FROST group has no secret that only its members hold for a recovery statement: $\mathsf{ask}$ is shared and never reconstructed, and $\mathsf{ak}$ is known to every viewer. ZIP 2005 avoids both by committing to a separate $\mathsf{qk} = \mathsf{H^{qk}}(\mathsf{qsk})$ whose secret is in no viewing key, and by having a FROST group derive $\mathsf{qsk}$ from a privately agreed value [ZIP-2005](#ref-zip2005). Tachyon has no counterpart, a regression from Ironwood both for FROST and for the separation of viewing from spending after the break.

## The suggested change

The fix is small. The proof-authorizing key becomes $(\mathsf{ak}, \mathsf{nk}, \mathsf{qk})$ with $\mathsf{qk} = \mathsf{H^{qk}}(\mathsf{qsk})$, ZIP 2005's BLAKE3 derivation, and the owner field $\mathsf{pk} = \mathsf{Com}(\mathsf{ak}, \mathsf{nk}, \mathsf{qk})$ stays one hash commitment: Tachyon's sponge absorbs four field elements a permutation and $\mathsf{pk}$ takes three, so $\mathsf{qk}$ fits as the fourth, entered as one Pallas element by reduction modulo $p$, since a 256-bit hash output is not one. The ordinary spend circuit is unchanged, since it opens $\mathsf{pk}$ as before with $\mathsf{qk}$ as one more field. The recovery statement proves knowledge of $\mathsf{qsk}$ and $\mathsf{nk}$ rather than of $\mathsf{ak}$ and $\mathsf{nk}$, and a FROST group derives $\mathsf{qsk}$ from its privately agreed value as ZIP 2005 prescribes, with $\mathsf{ak}$ still the distributed key generation's output. The cost is one more field in the commitment and, in the recovery proof, one BLAKE3 compression, <!--fig:app_qk_bits-->15,100<!--/fig--> Kishu digits, which the recovery circuit application already charges for $\mathsf{H^{qk}}$, and the reduction's range check of 256 bits.

## The migration transaction

A Tachyon note moves to Zoodles by a recovery spend and a Zoodles output. The transaction reveals the total it spends from the Tachyon pool, for the [pool accounting](zoodles.md#pool-accounting) that the Zoodles page sets out. The spend proves knowledge of $\mathsf{qsk}$ against $\mathsf{qk}$ in $\mathsf{pk}$, the Poseidon openings of $\mathsf{pk}$ and $\mathsf{cm}$ over Pallas, the membership of $\mathsf{cm}$ in a hash-based tree over Tachyon's published commitments, and the nullifier's derivation and its absence from Tachyon's published nullifiers. It cannot rely on Tachyon's set polynomials, which are Pedersen commitments, once discrete log is gone, so the tree is the structure for Tachyon's notes as it is for Zoodles' own epochs. The shape is ZIP 2005's Recovery Statement over Tachyon's note format, with Poseidon where Orchard has Sinsemilla. Pricing it as an application of the frontier page is pending; it is also why Tachyon's circuits are priced in each post-quantum proof system there.
