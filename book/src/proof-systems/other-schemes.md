# Other Schemes

The frontier page prices every candidate on the same measures; this section holds the longer
write-ups on the ones other than Kishu.
[Akita Proof Sizes at a Pasta Modulus](other-schemes/pasta-proof-size.md) asks what an Akita
opening would cost if the commitment modulus were Pallas's base field, which Tachyon and the
Ironwood recovery circuit want, and what a native Pasta profile of Akita would need.
[The Pasta-Claims Variant's Soundness Obligation](other-schemes/pasta-q64-soundness.md) states
the knowledge-soundness theorem that the Akita variant with Pasta claims over the 64-bit
commitment would need, since it decouples the commitment modulus from the claim field.

The hash-based families, WHIR, FRI, the 𝔽₂ family, and BitZ, and the Pedersen-IPA baseline have
no write-up of their own beyond the frontier page's
[hash-based](pcs-frontier.md#hash-based-families) and [Pedersen-IPA](pcs-frontier.md#pedersen-ipa)
sections.
